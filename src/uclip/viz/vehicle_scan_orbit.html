What this picture shows

The fixed PCA basis was fitted to 3,588 normalized image embeddings (1,404 Sim2 Jeep + 2,184 original sphere crops) from openai/clip-vit-base-patch16. Added Jeep-color features are projected through that same basis; changing series, elevation or display axes does not refit the space. The image and highlighted point always refer to the same cached embedding row. All cached points are shown by default, including repeated Sim2 captures at the same heading. Tick vehicles/colors independently under Vehicles on graph, or use Only inspected to focus on one series. All elevations shows every recorded view for each ticked series; turn it off to inspect one elevation. Heading paths connect views within each elevation ring, never across elevations. Changing the inspected image does not remove other comparison series. Choose the four viewpoint colors with the swatches at (0°, −5°), (0°, 90°), (180°, 90°), and (180°, −5°). Colors blend continuously between the four corners, retaining your exact chosen colors at the corners. Editing a swatch applies the same blend to the grid and PCA points. The default uses red, blue, green and yellow, with green at (0°, 90°) and yellow at (180°, −5°): opposite corners of the view grid. Presets provide starting colors. Custom colors persist in the view URL and this browser. Each PCA point uses the color at its recorded heading/elevation, shared across objects. Heading colors fold from 0° to 180° and back to 360°, where the color matches 0°. A 360° request selects the existing 0° image/vector. The archive has no underside view; the bottom row is the lowest recorded elevation (−5°). Sim2 uses an explicitly uncalibrated horizontal color band because its elevation is unknown. Vehicle / paint coloring remains available in the color selector.

Solid lines connect adjacent measured views separated by at most 5° in Sim2 or 15° in the scan grid. Dashed lines bridge missing angles and show the last-to-first wraparound as guides; they do not add images or embeddings. A 360° request returns the same sampled view as 0°. The data is not constrained to form a circle or a separated color cluster.

PCA distances use only the displayed axes. The cosine value uses the complete 512-dimensional vector, compared with a fixed front reference for that series (54° elevation for scans). These points represent image features, not uncertainty distributions or unique vehicle identities. The Sim2 elevation remains uncalibrated. The new scans have filename elevations −5°, 0°, 18°, 36°, 54°, 72° and 90°. Their heading convention is visually inferred: relative heading = (270 − filename azimuth) modulo 360. The first filename parameter, 10, has unconfirmed distance units. At the pole, azimuth describes image orientation while the viewing ray is vertical.

Camera-matched crop replay

These samples use the existing SECTR-C camera geometry, physical target bounds and exact trajectory samples. The closest recorded viewing ray is rotated into the camera axes, then isotropically downsampled and independently re-encoded. The turquoise diamond plots the selected camera crop’s independently encoded feature in the same PCA basis; its connector leads to the chosen native scan feature. Pixel crops and embeddings are retained; no ROS or MHT likelihood is changed.
